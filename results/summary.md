# Results: U.S. county population change, 2010-2019

Counties analyzed: 3,142

## 1. How evenly was growth spread across counties?
- The U.S. grew 6.1% from 2010 to 2019, yet 54% of counties (1,688 of 3,142) lost population.
- The 50 fastest-gaining counties (by number of people added) account for 49% of the country's net population gain.

## 2. Migration or births minus deaths?
- In 68% of counties, net migration moved population more than natural change (births minus deaths) did.
- 43% of counties had more deaths than births over the decade.
- 857 counties lost people both ways: more deaths than births and more people moving out than in.

## 3. Rural vs. urban counties
- Most rural: median change -2.6%, 35% of counties grew (629 counties)
- Rural: median change -3.2%, 29% of counties grew (628 counties)
- Middle: median change -1.2%, 39% of counties grew (628 counties)
- Suburban: median change +0.6%, 54% of counties grew (628 counties)
- Most urban: median change +4.8%, 75% of counties grew (629 counties)

## 4. Which county traits are linked to faster growth?
- Counties used: 3,109 (those with at least 1,000 residents and complete data)
- Correlation with growth, % adults with a bachelor's degree or more: r = +0.45
- Correlation with growth, % of residents 65 or older: r = -0.35
- Correlation with growth, Population density (log10): r = +0.31
- Correlation with growth, % foreign-born residents: r = +0.32
- A linear regression on these four traits explains 27% of the variation in county growth (5-fold cross-validated R^2 = 0.27).
- Standardized coefficients (percentage points of growth per 1 standard deviation):
  - % adults with a bachelor's degree or more: +2.89
  - % of residents 65 or older: -1.77
  - % foreign-born residents: +0.91
  - Population density (log10): +0.35
- These are associations, not causes: for example, college-educated workers may move to places that are already growing.

## 5. North Carolina
- NC grew 9.5% vs. 6.1% for the U.S., but 44 of its 100 counties lost population.
- Five Triangle counties (Wake, Durham, Orange, Chatham, Johnston) added 319,803 people: 35% of NC's total net gain.
- Fastest-growing NC counties: Brunswick (+32.2%), Johnston (+23.4%), Wake (+22.6%)
- Fastest-shrinking NC counties: Hyde (-15.0%), Washington (-11.7%), Northampton (-11.6%)
